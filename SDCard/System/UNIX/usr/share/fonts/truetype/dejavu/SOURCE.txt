DejaVu fonts 2.37

Unmodified TrueType fonts from the official DejaVu binary release:
https://github.com/dejavu-fonts/dejavu-fonts/releases/tag/version_2_37

Archive: dejavu-fonts-ttf-2.37.zip

Included families: DejaVu Sans, DejaVu Sans Mono, DejaVu Serif.

The accompanying LICENSE is copied verbatim from the release archive.
